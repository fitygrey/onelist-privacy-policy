# Rope Skipping Support

Rope Skipping works with iPhone and Apple Watch. You can record a workout automatically on Apple Watch or add a jump count manually on iPhone.

## Start an Apple Watch workout

1. Make sure the Rope Skipping app is installed on both your iPhone and Apple Watch.
2. Open the App on Apple Watch and tap **Start**.
3. The first tap may request Health and Motion & Fitness permissions. Complete the permission flow, then return to the App.
4. Tap **Start** again to enter the countdown and begin the workout.
5. Wear the watch securely and jump with a natural, consistent wrist movement.
6. End the workout on Apple Watch. Keep the iPhone and Apple Watch connected while the record synchronizes.

## Add a record manually

On iPhone, tap **Start Training**, then choose **Add Manually**. Enter the completed jump count, completion time, and optional duration.

Manual records do not contain measured heart rate or active energy and are not written to Apple Health.

## Change the daily goal

Open **Me → Daily Goal** on iPhone and choose your target. The App will synchronize the goal to Apple Watch when the devices are connected.

## Permissions

The Apple Watch recording feature may require:

- **Health:** to access workout heart rate and active energy, and to save a completed workout;
- **Motion & Fitness:** to recognize jump-rope motion and calculate the jump count.

You can review permissions in the Health app and in **Settings → Privacy & Security**. If you change a permission, reopen the App before trying again.

## Troubleshooting

### The watch does not count jumps

- Wear Apple Watch snugly, with the back of the watch touching your wrist;
- confirm Motion & Fitness permission is enabled;
- keep a natural wrist rhythm and avoid testing by shaking the watch only;
- restart the workout after changing permissions.

### Heart rate is not displayed

Heart rate may take a short time to appear after the workout starts. Wear the watch securely and confirm that Rope Skipping has permission to read heart rate in the Health app. A manual record does not include heart rate.

### A workout does not appear on iPhone

Keep Bluetooth and Wi-Fi enabled, keep the devices near each other, and open Rope Skipping once on both Apple Watch and iPhone. Records completed while temporarily disconnected are sent when the connection becomes available again.

### The Apple Watch option is unavailable

Open the Watch app on iPhone and confirm that Rope Skipping is installed on Apple Watch. Also confirm that the watch is paired with the same iPhone and is reachable.

### A workout is missing from Apple Health

Confirm that Health write permission is enabled for Rope Skipping. Records added manually on iPhone are not written to Apple Health.

### The count is inaccurate

Wear the watch consistently and avoid unusually loose straps or large non-jumping arm movements. If the issue continues, include the watch model, watchOS version, actual jump count, and displayed count when contacting support.
