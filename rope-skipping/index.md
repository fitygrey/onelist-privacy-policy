# Rope Skipping Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 1, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 1, 2026

Rope Skipping (the “App”) is provided by Jing Liu (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the iPhone and Apple Watch versions of the App.

## 1. Information processed by the App

The App processes the information needed to record and display your jump-rope activity, which may include:

- jump count, workout date and time, workout duration, pace, and daily goal progress;
- heart-rate and active-energy data available during an Apple Watch workout, when you grant the relevant Apple Health permission;
- workout source, such as Apple Watch automatic recording or a record you add manually;
- device connection and last-sync status used to show whether the iPhone and Apple Watch are communicating normally.

The App does not require you to create an account. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your workout records.

## 2. Apple Health and fitness permissions

The App requests system permissions only when they are needed for the feature you choose:

- **Health:** with your authorization, the Apple Watch app may read heart rate and active energy during a workout and save the completed workout to Apple Health;
- **Motion & Fitness:** used on Apple Watch to recognize jump-rope motion and calculate jump count;
- **Notifications:** if a notification feature is provided and enabled, used only to deliver the local notifications you choose.

You can review or change these permissions at any time in the Health app or in **Settings → Privacy & Security**.

Health information is used only to provide workout recording and display features. It is not used for advertising, and we do not sell Health information. A manually added jump count is stored as an App record and is not written to Apple Health.

## 3. Storage and Apple Watch synchronization

Workout records and goal settings are primarily stored locally on your iPhone. The App uses Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework to transfer necessary workout data and settings between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch.

This transfer is device-to-device through Apple’s system services. We do not receive the transferred content on our own server.

## 4. Analytics, advertising, and third-party services

The App does not include third-party advertising, third-party analytics, or a developer-operated cloud account service. We do not use your workout or Health data to build advertising profiles.

Apple system frameworks, including HealthKit, Core Motion, and WatchConnectivity, are subject to Apple’s platform privacy and security controls.

## 5. Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not intentionally disclose your workout or Health data to data brokers, advertisers, or marketing platforms.

Information may be disclosed only if required by applicable law, or when necessary to protect users, rights, or safety as permitted by law.

## 6. Retention and deletion

You can delete workout records in the App. Doing so removes the App’s local copy of those records.

Deleting an App record does not automatically delete a workout already saved in Apple Health. You can view and delete Health records separately in the Apple Health app.

Uninstalling the App removes data stored in that App’s local sandbox on the uninstalled device. Data previously written to Apple Health remains under your control in the Health app.

## 7. Children

If you are a minor, please use the App with the consent and guidance of your guardian.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, legal requirements, or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page and/or communicated in the App where appropriate.
